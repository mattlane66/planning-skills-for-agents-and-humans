# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and maintainers must make participation respectful, safe, and constructive. Welcome different experience levels and viewpoints; give actionable feedback; focus disagreement on ideas and evidence; and respect privacy, attribution, and informed consent.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, impersonation, plagiarism, or sustained disruption of project spaces.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other public or private spaces used to represent or coordinate the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/mattlane66). Do not include sensitive evidence in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect privacy where possible, and may edit or remove content, warn participants, limit participation, or ban contributors according to severity and pattern.

Retaliation against someone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation is prohibited.
