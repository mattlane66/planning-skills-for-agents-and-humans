# Simulation source audit findings

Independent review of the primary sources and available artifacts. The [coverage ledger](simulation-source-coverage.md) lists exact versions and unresolved inputs. The [benchmark results](simulation-benchmark-results.md) record subsequent execution checks. Specialized training and subject-matter procedures are tracked here without adding them to the general research workflow.

## S1. Social Simulacra

- **Verified public artifact.** Stanford links the [authors’ SimReddit demo](https://social-simulacra.herokuapp.com/). The public pages show pre-generated posts and replies, persona lists, and prompt disclosures. The [RidersRepublic example](https://social-simulacra.herokuapp.com/RidersRepublic) is another published generation. The [ACM SIGCHI video preview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xv8wwtDhj_I) is available. No runnable backend, complete evaluator data or randomization/config manifest was established. [Stanford publication record](https://hci.stanford.edu/publications/paper.php?id=467)
- **Generation details and limits.** §§3–4 specify behaviorally defined personas, default ten seeds expanded to 1,000, nudging through repeated natural-language/HTML instructions, a closing-tag stop, ancestor-conditioned replies, truncation of the oldest prefix, fresh author with probability 0.5 and alternate prior author otherwise, and a per-thread Gaussian continuation parameter centered at .65 with a reply cap. The paper leaves the Gaussian standard deviation and handling outside [0,1] unspecified and says “more than 8” replies, leaving stop indexing ambiguous. WhatIf modifies a speaker or intervention and preserves prior context. Multiverse samples alternatives (temperature .7 to .8) and can regenerate a full instance with different persona composition. §7 excludes ordinary platform actions such as votes and shares from the implementation, constrains it to text and English, and highlights false-negative and cultural-coverage risks. The PR covers the design pattern as workflow guidance. Exact branching and evaluation are still unimplemented locally. [Paper, §§3–4, 7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.04024v1)
- **Evaluation gaps.** §5 uses 50 English subreddits created after the source model release, two pairs per community, judges previewing current real threads, and four randomized between-subject arms of 50 judges each. Real candidate threads excluded the inspected recency/top feed, contained 3–5 text replies, and were compared to full generation, no personas, no context, and collaborative crowdworker generation. Table 1’s no-persona result is 34% misidentification and its full-model contrast has one star (p<.05); the prose says 33% and p<.01, and the caption overstates the pairwise threshold. Treat plausible appearance, designer decision changes, and observed deployment as distinct endpoints. §6 uses own-community before/after design review, think-aloud, three alternate replies before and after a user-written intervention, and two-stage inductive coding. The Lead User benchmark could additionally preserve coded changes, rejected prompts, and real counterexamples recalled by designers. Exact study replay needs the benchmark manifest, raw ratings, and historical model configuration. [Paper, §§5–7 and Table 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.04024v1)

## S2. Generative Agents

- **Archived trace inspected.** `compressed_storage/July1_the_ville_isabella_maria_klaus-step-3-20/master_movement.json` parses as 8,655 consecutive, zero-indexed ticks spanning three personas. Each emitted record carries movement, description, chat and an emoji field. `meta.json` accompanies the trace. This verifies the log structure and coverage of the deposited trace. The full experiment used 25 agents.

- **Static implementation verified.** `reverie.py:start_server` sequentially processes shared tile events, obtains each agent’s next position, and writes per-tick movement and dialogue records. `persona/cognitive_modules/perceive.py` limits recognition to nearby tiles in the current arena, filters recently seen event triples, and decrements the reflection budget using poignancy. The paper’s elapsed-hour memory decay and equal weights differ from ascending-rank .99 decay and .5/3/2 coefficients in `retrieve.py`; default top 30 is caller-dependent. `reflect.py` creates three focal questions, retrieves evidence, and asks for five insights with supporting memory IDs, then resets the 150-point budget in `scratch.py`. It also adds separate post-chat planning thoughts and memos. `plan.py` records daily plans, decomposes the near horizon on demand, and handles chat/wait reactions that update schedules. `converse.py:agent_chat_v2` alternates speakers for at most eight iterations, refreshing private relationship/memory and a four-utterance retrieval context. `execute.py` grounds plans in addresses, preferentially samples unoccupied target tiles and takes the shortest path among sampled tiles. [Paper, §§4–5 and Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.03442v2) · [Pinned implementation](https://github.com/joonspk-research/generative_agents/tree/fe05a71d3e4ed7d10bf68aa4eda6dd995ec070f4)
- **Missing evidence and checks.** Check tick order, memory permissions, incorrect-address and unreachable-path behavior, and schedule/duration conservation if adapting the implementation. Static code shows the missing-address branch reads a park address without setting `target_tiles`; the path routine can return a singleton end point after its search limit when blocked, without a separate failure signal. These are candidate conformance failures, not live observations. The README and repository contain three-agent seed and compressed movement replay artifacts. They do not establish the author’s specific two-day, 25-agent run, judgment rows, and source-matching model snapshots. Paper §6 uses a fixed full-architecture history, four architecture arms plus a human roleplay baseline, five probe categories, same-rater rankings and TrueSkill. Its Kruskal–Wallis/Dunn/Holm inferential comparison does not account for the within-rater design; a local evaluation should preserve the design with paired/blocked inference. §7 validates affirmative recall against specific dialogue, mutual acquaintance against memory logs, and coordination against timed physical attendance. Elicit negative recall as well as inspect positive support, and distinguish missed retrieval, fabricated receipt, and a stated plan that did not execute. The PR covers the core guidance and diagnostics. Reproduction needs the original run and rater data or new, explicitly separate empirical evaluation and a supported provider/model stack. [Paper, §§6–8 and Appendix B](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2304.03442v2)

## S3. AI Simulations Grounded in Rich Self-Reports Increase Accuracy and Reduce Distortion

- **Public recomputation verified.** The 14,147,932-byte public OSF archive matched the published SHA-256 `be73fce5b9f02c0dda784eb4bc7d178bd590bac9180d402af1fa978b2ff391bf`. Independent code reading the named public CSVs/JSON from the archive reproduced 13 GSS raw accuracies and all 42 Table 1 point comparisons with absolute error below 1e-12 against the deposited verification outputs. Results were 72.7738% combined, 69.4430% interview, 69.2890% survey, 60.4170% demographics, 59.6888% persona, and 79.5253% human two-week retest. Combined grounding closed the median 53.5439% of the demographic gap across eight measures. The hyper-rationality gap widened by 4.4696%. The public aggregation inputs already contain model-vs-human comparisons. This verifies arithmetic and provenance through those aggregates. [Paper, Figure 2 and Table 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.10109v4) · [Public OSF deposit](https://osf.io/t6g7k/files/osfstorage)
- **Interview and instrument additions.** SI §§1–8 and §§9.1, 9.20–9.22 distinguish independent instrument design, elapsed-time-controlled adaptive follow-ups with a fixed script, synthetic notes plus a rolling transcript window, a human-interviewer pilot, inductive evidence-retrieval coding, semantic overlap screening, and random lesion versus a lossy summary. GPT-4o used disciplinary reflections and an explicit four-step, option-by-option reasoning prompt. Opus 5.5 used tagged contexts and a minimal direct prompt, medium effort, blocks of at most four, whole-trait masks, and only assigned experiment conditions. A 64-person settings pilot overlapped the final 1,052; the sensitivity excluding those 64 changed GSS accuracy by at most 0.07 percentage points. Test prompt selection on independent participants when possible. Initial scatter in transcript–test overlap makes direct evidence, supported inference, and unavailable information separate validation strata. Keep response-range, item-join, unparseable-output and missingness checks explicit, as SI §9.21 records real pipeline corrections on each. [Paper, SI §§1–9.1, 9.20–9.22](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.10109v4)
- **Metrics and reliability additions.** The source’s Table 1 collapses six GSS items metrics at different scopes (mean absolute Glass delta, median SD ratio, median ordered Wasserstein, paired mean absolute Spearman ratio, first eigenvalue share after correlation-matrix regularization, and mean absolute response/ideology correlation), five experimental effect ratios, and one-sided excess toward game-specific earnings-maximizing behavior. `analysis/public_checks/recompute_distortions.py` makes regularization and targets explicit. The source’s normalized accuracy is a mean of per-person retest ratios, separate from attenuation-adjusted correlation or raw accuracy. Check null/small effects, divide-by-zero policy, empirical gap denominator, binary effects as Cohen’s h, and one within-subject effect powering. Add an external weighted human-distribution baseline and attrition comparability for generalization if the study can support them. Matching ordinal marginal responses can leave joint dependence erroneous (`joint_tables.py` iterative proportional fitting). Calibration and resolution remain separate (`brier_analysis.py`). The PR specifies adapted metrics, with basic descriptive metrics executable. Specialized source-level inference, full item recoding, and predictive evaluation remain open. [Paper, SI §§5, 9.5–9.19](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.10109v4)
- **Preregistration verified against original record.** The OSF registration was filed 2024-06-05. Public API responses q2/q6/q10/q13–q17/q20–q22 substantiate the preregistered H1–H3, wave 2 benchmark, instruments, quota variable, size/stopping rules, ratios, hypothesis tests, wave-1-only eligibility, and the planned fraud classifier. SI Table S31 and §10.2 disclose ten deviations. There were two added grounding arms, changed headline metric, 27 overlapping items dropped, repeated-measures/paired checks added, revised H3 covariate coding and missingness, new clipping and undefined-r rules, analysis limited to two-wave completers, two participants starting pre-registration, party replacing ideology in quota sampling, and repaired experiment effect-size/wave conventions. Table S31 also flags the exploratory within-person economic-game analysis beyond q15. The preregistration distinguishes eligible wave-1 analysis (q20–21) from recruitment stopping at ~1,000 complete participants (q13). The tests used in the paper for paired comparisons should use their independent-unit structure locally. §10.4 lists additional exploratory analyses. [Original OSF registration](https://osf.io/gk45t/) · [Public registration API](https://api.osf.io/v2/registrations/gk45t/) · [Paper, SI §§9.3 and 10](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.10109v4)
- **Specific replication barriers.** The archive’s `ARTIFACTS.md` maps each figure/table to aggregate output, scripts and prerequisites. Person-level human/model fixed-instrument responses, correctness, scores, demographic fields and pairings require author-reviewed research access and a Stanford institutional data-use agreement. Raw transcripts, interviews/summaries, reflections, self-descriptions, provider calls, raw free text, LoRA training code and adapters are unreleased. Even restricted tables do not regenerate original transcript-built simulations or SI Figures S5–S6’s side analysis. The original GPT-4o pipeline is partly preserved as aggregate outputs, results/configurations and scoring paths, with some scored free text withheld and composite/demographic run mapping limitations described in `analysis/public_checks/recompute_accuracy.py`. The attrition output is public but its input and some upstream code are unreleased. Source-reported confidence intervals need the restricted paired records. I inspected the archive manifest and selected harness/metric paths and the relevant appendix sections. A line-by-line review of every deposited script and every table cell was not performed. [OSF deposit, `README.md`, `DATA_TERMS.md` and `ARTIFACTS.md`](https://osf.io/t6g7k/files/osfstorage)

## S6 — Maier et al., SSR, v3

[Paper and appendices](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.08338v3) and [reference package](https://github.com/pymc-labs/semantic-similarity-rating/tree/86dcd2597c7824e4fd6546b884c5500c43a4b022).

- §§3–4, App. A.4 and Figs. 9–31 compare direct numeric, free text then same-model follow-up numeric, and free text then SSR, with matched demographic stimulus conditions, repeats and elicitation temperature separate from rating temperature. App. B compares image-containing and image-transcribed text stimuli. App. C and Fig. 32 describe six five-anchor reference sets, embedding choice and parameter sensitivity. §5 says the purchase-intent anchors were manually optimized for the same 57 surveys. Do not treat as independent anchor evaluation.
- §4.4 and Figs. 33–35 separately test concept relevance, a different outcome with three newly authored anchor sets; FLR gave stronger attainment in this example, SSR stronger distributional fit. Include separate construct benchmarks and allow trade-offs. Age, income, gender, region, ethnicity, category and concept source are stratification/ablation slices; the paper’s §5 subgroup caveats matter.
- App. A.3 Eqs. 3–6 average per-survey KS similarity, compare ordered KS to distribution cosine, calculate Pearson on concept means, and divide *average* human–synthetic split-half correlation by *average* human–human split-half correlation. Match cohort sizes, use 2,000 randomized comparisons in the study, and report raw correlations and the denominator. This is split-half attainment with independent people within each concept, distinct from longitudinal consistency, individual accuracy or calibrated behavioral validity. App. D repeats a 28-concept train/29-concept test LightGBM comparison 300 times with nested respondent splits and specific demographic/concept features. App. E’s output comparisons are qualitative illustrations. These are benchmark procedures, not implemented by the current person-grouped KS/TVD holdout.
- Eqs. 7–9 use raw cosine, subtract the within-set minimum, add epsilon to one minimum, normalize, power-scale, and average across sets. App. A.4.3 uses epsilon 0 and rating T=1 for its core results. Upstream `compute.py` rescales cosine to [0,1], so matching a raw-cosine epsilon requires halving it in upstream units. Upstream `ResponseRater.get_response_pmfs('mean')` and `pmfs_from_similarities('mean')` average untempered anchor-set PMFs then apply temperature. `scaled/simulation/ssr.py::measure` applies temperature before averaging. Nonunit T with multiple sets makes these operations noncommutative. Record the convention and verify both with a two-set fixture if claiming parity. Paper describes temperature with per-set indices; at the published core T=1 the order agrees. Upstream default text mode uses all-MiniLM-L6-v2; the study used OpenAI text-embedding-3-small. Upstream T=0 is a special argmax operation; local code requires T>0 and rejects all-equal anchors in its paper branch. These are behavior differences; preserve already versioned legacy artifacts.
- The reference library implements the arithmetic and text/embedding interface, while the local scaled route implements the guarded transform, response hashes and an aggregate/sensitivity summary. The Behavioral Simulation compatibility command is legacy single-set. No head-to-head elicitation, cross-concept benchmark, LightGBM run, complete production anchor/prompt package, or permissioned benchmark files were established in the inspected repository.

## S7 — Krsteski et al., Valid Survey Simulations; PPI++

[ACL 2026](https://aclanthology.org/2026.acl-long.498.pdf), [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.11408v2), [replication repo](https://github.com/skrsteski/survey-simulations/tree/d0926093b57b373760679c4b6d7ef01d4447c387), [PPI++ v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.01453v2), and [library](https://github.com/aangelopoulos/ppi_py/tree/3d1f0c668444907b39bd045cb9dd38e479ce7dd6).

- Both versions use the same §5 Table 2 headline values. ACL adds App. A.5 Twin2k-500 with prior same-item response as a strong baseline and a case of small ESS gain despite rectification, plus concrete App. B.4 fine-tuning configurations and Table 10 general estimating equations. Both have App. A.1 individual MAE/identical-profile heterogeneity, A.2 label-count/ESS curves, A.3 covariate blocks and single-/multi-turn × conditioning ablation (potential acquiescence), A.4 per-model outcomes and failed near-constant predictions. Preserve repeat/naïve and random baselines; evaluate label counts and keep empirical individual error separate from population performance. §5.2/Table 3 applies global re-centering to study subgroup error, including a worsening subgroup, not subgroup inference; Fig. 2 sweeps training vs correction with fixed budget, uncertainty and Pareto comparison.
- Main Eqs. 1–3 and PPI++ §6 support lambda multiplying both prediction terms, so zero is human-only. App. B.1 in both versions incorrectly scales only the rectifier and describes zero as pure imputation. ACL Table 10 prints a logistic rectifier with opposite sign to the correction implied by its prediction score; use the upstream estimator, not that table. The centered estimating equation is unbiased under its conditions; exact finite-sample point unbiasedness of arbitrary roots does not follow. The fixed/disjoint-pilot mean and variance in `behavioral-simulation/scripts/scarce_evidence.py` are narrower, with no confidence intervals. Same-label adaptive PPI++, general M-estimation, survey weighting, finite-population designs, dependence and CIs still require their own procedures and empirical checks.
- Both App. B.2s describe ascending ordinal A→1, B→2 and so on. Repository README and `datasets/atp*/evaluate.py` use Q1 A–E→4–0, Q2 A–D→4–1, and remove uncertain/refused values. An affine recoding on identical rows preserves absolute error and variance/ESS, whereas relative error generally changes after an offset/reversal; treating an option as missing also changes the analysis sample. The evaluators inner-join predictions and can yield method-specific target means and valid frames. Retain an explicitly frozen common spine and report refusals for fresh comparisons.
- `src/synrec/metrics.py` repeatedly samples labeled subsets *without replacement* from one target wave; the unlabeled group is the complement. Each method advances the global RNG separately. These are trial splits conditional on a fixed frame, not an independent-wave test. The wrapper `ppi.py::ppi_mean_ci_with_var` uses adaptive library tuning and ddof=0 in its PPI variance decomposition, while the human comparator uses ddof=1. `reproduce.py` covers three core tasks, 100 repeated splits, and the main table. It does not compute LLM-only coverage, and prints ESS even for rows the paper suppresses for poor coverage. The archive README reports that some outputs were regenerated following storage loss. These distinctions matter to claimed numerical parity.
- Full input-to-output reproduction is blocked in the pinned checkout. Both ATP `simulate.py` files refer to an untracked `data/atp/human_test.csv` path and utility symbols absent from `src/synrec/utils.py`. NHANES `simulate.py` imports an absent `synrec.retrieval`, missing utilities, starts from an external prepared dataset with persona/one-shot fields, and refers to unavailable `data/fndds/` files. It contains a more elaborate two-pass food-component grounding/search route than the concise paper B.2 mapping description. Prepared author CVs/outputs and accessible inputs support a fresh authorized audit of the core estimates; appendix tables, allocation/subgroup scripts, exact regenerated raw draws and all synthesis/training steps need further inputs, author materials or compute. The source reviewer inspected code and filenames. The separate execution check in the benchmark results replays one archived model.
