# Research basis and limits

Source access checked 2026-11-26. The adaptations below are proposed for Lead User Research. Their effectiveness as a combined method remains untested. Read the original studies before transporting a numerical result to a different task.

| ID | Primary source and access | Tested task, comparison, measure and limitation | Adaptation and boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| LU-1986 | von Hippel, [Lead Users: A Source of Novel Product Concepts](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.32.7.791), July 1986. Verified publisher paper and author manuscript. | Methodological argument and illustrations of emerging need and early user innovation. Section 3 defines the two attributes. | Trend and expected benefit govern episode qualification. No validation of agent qualification or population demand. |
| LU-3M | Lilien et al., [Performance Assessment of the Lead User Idea-Generation Process for New Product Development](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.48.8.1042.171), August 2002. Verified primary abstract. | Natural experiment within 3M, lead-user versus contemporaneous traditional projects. Abstract reports average five-year projected annual sales of $146 million and over eight times comparator forecasts. These are forecasts in one company. | Retain analogous markets and assess opportunity separately. No realized sales estimate or universal multiplier. |
| PAIRS | von Hippel and von Krogh, [CROSSROADS—Identifying Viable “Need–Solution Pairs”: Problem Solving Without Problem Formulation](https://doi.org/10.1287/orsc.2015.1023), 2016 issue, published online 2015. Verified publisher full text. | Conceptual theory with examples. Discussion proposes future empirical work. No comparative trial or performance metric. | Preserve open pair-led search and controlled reformulation. No claim of greater effectiveness. |
| SOCIAL | Luo, Liu and Cui, [Identifying Lead Users on Social Media to Foster Digital Innovation: A Generative AI Approach](https://aisel.aisnet.org/icis2025/diginnoventren/diginnoventren/10/), December 2025. Verified abstract, full text inaccessible (403). | Short paper combines semantic and behavioral features on Reddit. Abstract supports feasibility. Sample, comparator and metrics unavailable from that abstract. | Use triage to find originals and audit misses. No automated episode qualification or prevalence. |
| MINING | [Mining novel customer needs for product design from user-generated content through large language model-enabled data augmentation and ensemble learning](https://doi.org/10.1080/00207543.2026.2625971), 2026. Provisional, indexed title verified, publisher full text blocked. | Task suggested by title. Sample, comparator and metrics unverified. | Rare-case probes and low-rank inspection are proposals. No performance claim. |
| ANALOG | [Human analogical guidance amplifies LLM performance through cross-domain knowledge activation](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-70873-7), 2026. Provisional, indexed publisher and PubMed records, full text blocked. | Benchmarked cross-domain knowledge activation. Read the full methods before carrying any numerical result forward. | Map functional relation and mismatch before using analogs. No proof of transfer in a product setting. |
| DIVERSITY | Terwiesch et al., [Artificial intelligence and its impact on creativity and diversity: An empirical study of large language model-generated product ideas](https://doi.org/10.1177/10591478261474243), 2026. Verified publisher article. | Product-idea generation experiments compare human and LLM methods, individual ideas and sets. Novelty and similarity of generated text provide limited evidence about functional variety or product success. | Separated branches and causal deduplication are proposed. No evidence of independent human agreement or field efficacy. |
| NULLS | Bao et al., [Contemporary AI lacks the imagination to diverge or negate in science](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.08251v3), 10 August 2026, v3. Verified preprint, Methods and supplementary A.4–A.6. | Abstract and Fig. 1 report 6,749 scientists and 25,139 rating sets, comparing model hypotheses with human-authored hypotheses drawn from recent papers. Tested models rarely explored the space occupied by nulls and did not spontaneously propose nulls in the tested setup. Automated judges had weak agreement with experts. Survey selection and scientific tasks limit transfer. | Record a meaningful null or rival and decide what observable evidence would discriminate. No claim that all models or prompts fail or that method improves product discovery. |
| CITATIONS | Asai et al., [Synthesizing scientific literature with retrieval-augmented language models](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-10072-4), 4 February 2026. Verified full text, ScholarQABench, citation evaluation and Methods. | Long-form scientific Q&A compared retrieval, feedback and expert/system baselines using citation accuracy, coverage and human judgments. Coverage depends on available open-access sources. | Inspect precise source spans and audit citation–claim entailment separately from citation existence. Citation entailment alone cannot establish truth. |
| CRITIQUE | Gottweis et al., [Accelerating scientific discovery with Co-Scientist](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-10644-y), May 2026. Verified full text, architecture, examples and Discussion. | Iterative proposal, critique, retrieval and ranking across selected biomedical cases, with selected external checks. Open-access and negative-result coverage are limited, and hypothesis validation is selective. | Timebox critique, retrieve and revise around a consequential objection. Ranking/model votes are neither independent evidence nor truth. |
| FIELD | [Experimental Comparison of AI-Enabled Semi-Structured Interviews and Fixed Surveys: Respondent Satisfaction, Response Patterns, Quality, and Representation](https://doi.org/10.1093/poq/nfag063), 2026. Verified article, experimental design and Results. | Methods report 2,243 opt-in participants randomly assigned across five modes on CloudResearch Connect. Compares satisfaction, open and closed responses, representation and quality. Mode can change responses. Convenience sampling limits generalization. | Record exact probes, separate spontaneous from prompted statements, preserve an ordinary observation or human interviewer route. No proof a voice interviewer improves lead-user research. |
| EXPERIMENT | [Large language models as uncertainty-calibrated optimizers for experimental discovery](https://doi.org/10.1038/s42256-026-01283-z), 2026. Provisional, PubMed metadata and abstract, full text unavailable in the accessible route. | Tests experimental discovery optimization in defined tasks. Do not import unverified comparators or calibration metrics. | Prefer discriminating observations worth their cost. No automated intervention ranking or transferable calibration. |
| SIMULATION | Park et al., [AI Simulations Grounded in Rich Self-Reports Increase Accuracy and Reduce Distortion](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.10109v4), 16 November 2026, v4. Verified PDF, abstract, Fig. 2 and distortion analysis. | Abstract and Methods report 1,052 US adults. Demographic descriptions, self-authored personas, surveys, interviews and combined grounding were compared on held-out surveys and behavior. Rich self-report improved survey prediction and reduced several distortions, with no corresponding improvement for incentivized economic choices. | Compare permissioned grounding with simple baselines, test distortion and retain extremes. No claim about switching, purchasing or causal intervention effects. |
| SSR | Maier et al., [LLMs Reproduce Human Purchase Intent via Semantic Similarity Elicitation of Likert Ratings](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.08338v3), 2025, v3. Verified preprint, Methods and Table 1. | 57 personal-care concept surveys (9,300 U.S. human responses, Methods §4) compared five-point stated-intent distributions, relative concept ranking and direct elicitation. Proprietary category-specific sample. | External optional elicitation with fixed anchors, distributions and sensitivity checks. Stated intent cannot establish behavior or adoption. |
| GEN | [StanfordHCI/genagents](https://github.com/StanfordHCI/genagents), repository at commit `96854071ef4c2d79c93144c973c7820722d52bab`, verified repository reference. | Implementation reference for grounding and structured responses. Park v4 supplies measured results. | Permissioned source-linked memories and compact vs rich grounding comparisons. No imported agent bank or mandatory reflection. |

The earlier [empirical enhancements](empirical-enhancements.md) and [need–solution discovery](need-solution-discovery.md) remain part of the method. Their search and scale findings concern particular populations, and their evaluation proposals remain prospective.

## Prior refinements rechecked

| ID | Primary source and access | Evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| SCREEN | Stockstrom et al., [Identifying Valuable Users as Informants for Innovation Processes: Comparing the Search Efficiency of Pyramiding and Screening](https://pure.au.dk/portal/en/publications/identifying-valuable-users-as-informants-for-innovation-processes/), 2016. Author manuscript indexed. | The comparison concerns referral efficiency in a bounded school network. It motivates adaptive referrals and independent branches, with no conclusion about product success. |
| NETWORK | Kratzer et al., [The Social Network Position of Lead Users](https://doi.org/10.1111/jpim.12291), 2016. Publisher and author manuscript indexed. | Network placement can help choose referral seeds. Network coverage and the edge definition matter; centrality supplies neither qualification attribute. |
| ANALOG-LU | Franke, Poetz and Schreier, [Integrating Problem Solvers from Analogous Markets in New Product Ideation](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2013.1805), 2014. Publisher and author manuscript indexed. | Raters distinguished novel from immediately usable ideas when comparing participants from focal and analogous markets. Carry both questions separately. |
| SCALE | Paulus, Jordanow and Heidenreich, [Measuring Lead Userness: Development and Validation of a Hierarchical Scale](https://doi.org/10.1111/caim.70006), 2025. Primary institutional record verified. | Scale validation concerns measured respondent constructs. Preserve prior optional diagnostic and its limits. Use the published instrument and check Table 2 before transporting its numerical comparisons. |
| SCIENTIFIC | Camuffo et al., [A scientific approach to entrepreneurial decision-making: Large-scale replication and extension](https://doi.org/10.1002/smj.3580), 2024. Primary article and working paper indexed. | The replication compares a theory-led approach across entrepreneurial experiments and records termination and pivoting. Use it to design falsification and stop rules while evaluating this method independently. |
| CLASSIFY | Fang et al., [A machine learning and clustering-based methodology for the identification of lead users and their needs from online communities](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eswa.2024.123381), 2024. Primary publisher abstract. | Online-community classification motivates triage and manual inspection of misses. Source selection and generalization remain open. |
| DIFFUSION | Schweisfurth and Dharmawan, [Does lead userness foster idea implementation and diffusion? A study of internal shopfloor users](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.respol.2018.08.018), 2019. Primary publisher and author manuscript. | Internal shopfloor implementation and diffusion warrant actual behavioral traces and lineage, without treating observed associations as causal transfer. |
| RELATIONSHIP | De Jong et al., [Industrial product development with lead users as a source of Schumpeterian opportunity](https://doi.org/10.1111/jpim.12739), 2024. Primary publisher record. | The relationship among lead userness, relationship length and radical opportunity is observational. Search beyond established customers and compare constraints. |

Metadata and abstracts support these narrow adaptations. Keep unresolved sample,
comparator and numerical details provisional until the original methods and
tables can be checked. The contest and causal-theory working papers listed in
the prior enhancement reference add no performance claims to this revision.
