# Lead User Research — Research Input

Fill in what you know. Leave a field blank rather than guessing.

```text
Research Domain / Problem Space:
[what space are we investigating?]

Target Market:
[who or what market is in scope?]

What do we want to understand?
[the research question / learning objective]

What human decision should this research help inform?
[the decision the evidence should improve]

Desired innovation altitude:
[need / workflow / product category / system / other]

Optional hypotheses:
[ideas to test, not assumptions to prove]

Mode:
SCOUT | STANDARD | FULL

Optional seed sources / experts:
[links, names, communities, files]

Optional constraints:
[time, geography, language, privacy, source types]
```

Use **STANDARD** when unsure.

If only the domain and decision are known, start anyway. Phase A may draft the missing brief fields, but every drafted field must be labeled **PROVISIONAL** and surfaced rather than silently treated as user-provided.
