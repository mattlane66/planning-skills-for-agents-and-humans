What to remember

  1. x→f()→yIt’s a claim. Test it; don’t assume it.
  2. Ec·EeTwo different judgments. Conformance asks whether the design—and later the built thing—meets R under M. Effect asks whether reality moved toward y and what supports attribution.
  3. x · y · M → RFrame without the answer. Keep the reason, model the operating world explicitly, and give every requirement a source.
  4. ✓ ✗ ?Compare real options. An unknown isn’t a pass, a missed must-have can’t be averaged away, and conformance alone does not choose the investment.
  5. vs.Judge effect against a comparison, and check who got measured.
  6. ← ← ←Diagnose backward before you blame the build.

Build forward. Verify locally. Diagnose backward. Correct against reality.
