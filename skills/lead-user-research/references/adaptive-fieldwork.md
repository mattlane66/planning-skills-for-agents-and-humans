# Adaptive fieldwork

Use real interviews when the observability record identifies a consequential
gap that available artifacts, logs or behavior cannot resolve. Use a human
interviewer when sensitivity, stakes or the possibility of interviewer influence
warrant it. Obtain informed consent for collection, recording and any model
processing. Ask for the minimum dates, roles and artifacts needed to reconstruct
the episode. Restricted material stays within the authorized research setting.

1. Ask the participant for one specific recent or consequential episode.
2. Reconstruct it in time order. Ask what they encountered, tried, changed,
   abandoned and used next. Ask which artifact or behavior might confirm the account.
3. Give the participant space to volunteer what mattered. Follow up neutrally
   on missing observations and alternatives. For example, “What happened next?”
   and “Can you recall an instance where you did the same thing with a different outcome?”
4. Preserve the exact question and sequence around a probed statement. Mark
   SPONTANEOUS or PROBED. Keep the participant's statement, direct observation
   and analyst interpretation distinct. Track ambiguities and contradictions.
5. Record UNKNOWN when evidence is unavailable. Stop after the information needed
   for the decision is collected, permission ends or further inquiry is disproportionate.

Avoid leading or persuasive prompts and invented motives. An interviewer suggestion
is not a participant statement. A participant account can describe behavior; an
observer or artifact is required to label it directly observed. Selective follow-ups,
modality and AI interviewer effects can change answers. Maintain an independent
behavioral or artifact check where consequential. See [methodology evidence](methodology-evidence.md).
